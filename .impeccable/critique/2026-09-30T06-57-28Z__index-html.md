## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: Strongly product-specific, not category-interchangeable. The four-state evidence chips, the status-record panel that deliberately *replaces* a photograph for the contracted project, BERC-tariff citations, and the mono numbered wayfinding system are all things only this company could publish. The solar category defaults (green leaves, panel close-ups, gradient glow) are conspicuously absent, per BRAND.md. Structural sameness risk is low; the one place the site drifts toward generic is the entry portal's duplicated scroll instruction, which reads as vestigial rather than authored.

**Deterministic scan**: CLI detector ran in degraded regex mode (htmlparser2/css-tree unavailable → undercount): 1 finding, `src/pages/home.html:219 <img .../>` — **false positive**, that line is an HTML comment documenting the photo-substitution rule, not a tag. Browser detector (injected `detect.js`, live console read) found 9 rule groups on the home page: `tiny-text` (11px section indices), `wide-tracking` (0.08em on mono labels), `line-length` (89/96/**151** chars), `all-caps-body`, `cramped-padding` (`proofbar__cell` ×3), `undersized-ui-text` (10px tags, **9px** table headers), `tight-leading` (1.02x — display heading, false positive), `kicker-above-heading` ×8, `em-dash-overuse` (**45 in body text**), `cream-palette` (`--paper #F5F3EF`). Cross-referencing BRAND.md: all-caps mono labels, kicker/sec-head pattern, numbered labels, and the warm paper background are **documented deliberate brand decisions** — not findings. ## Overall Impression

A disciplined, unusually honest marketing site: the content system (evidence states, status vocabulary, cited figures) is out-of-category craft. The biggest gap between it and 10/10 is small-debris accumulation on the *entry* experience and responsive edge: a duplicated instruction on the very first screen, a 1px overflow that breaks the README's own QA contract on half the routes, and a tail of code/doc drift that keeps the audit scores capped.

## What's Working

- **Evidence discipline as design**: chips + the four-state definition table turn credibility into a visual system. No competitor in this category does this.
- **The record-panel-not-photo decision**: withholding imagery for the un-built asset is a design move that reads as confidence, and it's executed without leaving a hole in the layout.
- **Form as a reference implementation**: inline errors, focus-first-error, aria-invalid/describedby, three honest handoff paths, no fake success.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Duplicate "Scroll to enter" on the portal entry.** What: two spans render the same instruction simultaneously (`glyph-portal.html:49` `gp__scroll` "Scroll to enter ↓" and `:53` `data-gp-hint` "Scroll to enter."). Why it matters: the first screen every visitor sees contradicts itself — two cues for one action reads as breakage and dilutes the "Step inside" affordance. Fix: delete the `.gp__scroll` span and its two CSS rules (site.css:1203-1204); the caption pair (hint + Step inside) carries every state, including reduced-motion where the hint is already hidden. Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`
- **[P1] 1px horizontal overflow at 390px on `/`, `/projects`, `/about`.** What: `documentElement.scrollWidth` = 391 vs 390 clientWidth, traced to decorative absolutes `.record::before` (inset −7%, 392.16px) and `.pagehero--media::after` (444.6px) whose ancestors don't clip. Why it matters: breaks README's explicit "no horizontal overflow at 390px on any route" contract; causes lateral jitter on mobile. Fix: `overflow-x: clip` on the owning content container (`main`), which preserves the intentional −7% bleed up to the viewport edge. Suggested command: `$impeccable adapt`
- **[P2] Line length + proofbar density.** What: one full-width paragraph measures ~151 chars/line (target <80); `proofbar__cell` children sit flush against the cell background with no inset (3 cells). Why it matters: the 151-char paragraph is the only genuinely hard-to-read body text on the page; flush proof cells look unfinished at the hero-to-content transition. Fix: constrain the offending paragraph with the existing `maxw-48`/`lead` pattern; add the standard cell inset to `.proofbar__cell`. Suggested command: `$impeccable layout`
- **[P2] Code/doc drift capping QUALITY/SPEC.** What: `DL.marquee`, `DL.tabs`, `DL.maskedVideoHero` run on every load with zero markup hooks; `const dark` (core.js:66) unread; `.gp__copy/title/lead/cat` CSS orphaned; README documents a removed hero toggle, a fake-`setTimeout` form, and a dead `DL.parallax`; estimate OG card points at `how-it-works.jpg`. Why it matters: every future audit re-discovers the same drift; SPEC can't score while the spec of record is false. Fix: delete dead primitives + orphan CSS, truth-up README, point estimate OG at its own card. Suggested command: `$impeccable polish`
- **[P3] Em-dash count and 9px headers.** What: 45 em-dashes in body copy; portfolio table `th` renders at 9px (site tags are already 10px, detector floor is 11px). Why it matters: the voice rules say "short declaratives" — the dash density softens them; 9px uppercase is at the edge of legibility for the site's own audience on mobile. Fix: comma/period pass on the heaviest paragraphs; raise `th` to the site's mono-label size. Suggested command: `$impeccable typeset`

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (First-Timer)**: The entry portal shows *two* identical "Scroll to enter" cues plus "Step inside" — three competing entry signals where one decision is expected; hesitation on the first screen. Footer labels ("Estimate your saving") vs nav ("Estimate") for the same route are close enough to read as two different features.

**Casey (Distracted Mobile)**: 1px overflow on 3 of 6 routes = subtle lateral jitter while thumb-scrolling a 4000px page; breaks the "one-handed, interrupted" flow. The proof strip packs 5 information chunks above the fold — dense but survives because it's the documented proof hierarchy.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent)**: Skips link works, focus rings are real (`:focus-visible` with 2px outline), form errors announce via `aria-describedby` — but the 9px table headers and 10px tags are below comfortable low-vision thresholds, and the duplicated scroll cue means the non-aria-hidden instance is the *centered* one while the caption's hint is `aria-hidden`, so SR users hear one cue and sighted users see two.
