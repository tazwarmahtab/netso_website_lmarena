How to read this page Evidence standard

We only publish what we can stand behind.

Everything Netso publishes about its projects carries one of four evidence states. We use them because the difference between an installed asset and an idea matters — to customers, to lenders, and to anyone reading this page.

Verified

Confirmed by contract, commissioning record or third-party documentation. Publishable as fact.

Corroborated

Supported by more than one credible source, though not yet formally documented.

Interpretive

Our reading of the market, the model or an opportunity. Labelled as a view, not a fact.

Unresolved

Known to be incomplete or awaiting confirmation. We do not present these as operating results.

PPA signed · pre-construction

An 80 kWp rooftop system contracted with Chittagong Grammar School under a signed Power Purchase Agreement.

Netso originated the project, surveyed the roof and structured the commercial case against the school's own electricity bill: the contracted tariff sits roughly a third below the BERC grid benchmark. Under the agreement Netso finances, owns and operates the system, and the school buys the electricity it produces. Construction has not yet started.

Indicative pricing

The contracted first asset prices roughly a third below the BERC grid benchmark of Tk 15.36 per kWh (June 2026 tariff order). The tariff band across projects still in screening is Tk 11.50–12.00 per kWh. That band is indicative, not an offer: every tariff is set after survey, metering and load analysis, and capacity, yield and price are confirmed before a PPA is signed. Not every roof qualifies.
