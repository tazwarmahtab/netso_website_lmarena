02 — The rooftop opportunityThe gap is ownership

A commercial roof is already an energy site. It just has no owner yet.

Bangladesh’s commercial rooftops are stranded assets — from RMG and textile plants outside Dhaka to industrial estates around Chattogram. The businesses underneath them pay a rising grid bill, while the roof stays outside the core capital plan.

The choices today are familiar: stay on the grid, run diesel backup, or self-build a solar system and tie up capital in a non-core asset. Netso creates the missing fourth option.
