Privacy Policy

How Netso Energy handles information you share with us.

Last updated — 2026

This policy describes how Netso Energy ("Netso", "we") collects and uses information through this website and in the course of assessing and developing energy projects.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide. When you submit a project enquiry we collect your name, company, email address, phone number, facility details (location, type, rooftop area, electricity consumption, tariff, sanctioned load, existing generation) and any additional information you choose to include.
  • Correspondence. Records of the emails, calls and meetings that follow an enquiry.
  • Technical information. Basic technical data generated by your browser when you visit the site, such as pages requested and approximate location derived from your IP address, used for security and to understand how the site is used.

How we use it

  • To assess whether a facility can support a viable energy project and to respond to your enquiry.
  • To prepare project proposals, feasibility assessments and commercial terms.
  • To communicate with you about an enquiry, a project or a partnership.
  • To meet legal, regulatory and contractual obligations, including obligations to financing parties where a project reaches that stage.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not publish counterparties, facility details, capacities or commercial terms for projects that have not reached contract.
  • We do not use facility information for any purpose other than assessing and developing the project you contacted us about, unless we have your agreement.

Sharing

Information may be shared with professional advisers, contractors, equipment suppliers and financing parties strictly as required to assess, develop, build or operate a project, and only on a confidential basis. We may disclose information where we are legally required to do so.

Retention

Enquiry and project information is kept while it is needed for the purposes described above, and for as long as any agreement that results from it requires. Specific retention periods, storage locations and the company’s data-protection point of contact are being finalised and will be published on this page.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Write to hello@netso.energy and we will respond within a reasonable period.

Cookies

This site does not set advertising or profiling cookies. Any cookies used are limited to those required for the site to function and, if enabled, aggregate analytics. Where analytics are introduced, this page will say so.

Changes

We may update this policy as the business develops. The current version will always be published on this page.
