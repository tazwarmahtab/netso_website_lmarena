What happens next

01

Initial review

We read the details and check whether the roof and the load could support a project — usually within one working week.

02

Site assessment

If it looks viable, we arrange a survey: roof structure, layout, access and consumption profile.

03

Indicative proposal

We come back with a project concept, commercial structure and the terms we would want to discuss.

What makes a strong candidate

  • Large, flat or low-slope roof, structurally sound
  • Meaningful daytime electricity consumption
  • Commercial or industrial tariff
  • Owner or long lease on the site
  • Willingness to commit to a long-term supply agreement

Missing information is not a problem. Send what you have — roof area, a recent bill, or just the facility address — and we’ll take it from there.

Confidentiality

Project information shared with Netso is treated as confidential and used only to assess and develop the opportunity. We don’t publish counterparties, capacities or commercial terms for projects that have not reached contract.
