Evidence before scale

Projects are disclosed by evidence stage.

Netso does not use a development-stage conversation as a portfolio claim. When a project is ready to be public, this page will show what has been assessed, contracted, built and measured — separately.

The disclosure ladderNo manufactured proof

Each claim earns its stage.

A rooftop opportunity can be real without being ready for a logo, a capacity figure or an operating claim. This is the standard Netso uses to keep those statements distinct.

  1. 01

    Screened

    Roof, load and counterparties are being assessed. This is an opportunity, not a project announcement.

  2. 02

    Contracted

    A Power Purchase Agreement and the relevant commercial approvals exist. Public copy names the stage and only the facts approved for disclosure.

  3. 03

    Under construction

    Physical work has begun. Progress claims are tied to an observable construction record, not an intention to build.

  4. 04

    Operating

    Performance claims follow metered generation data and a defined reporting period. Estimates never become operating facts by repetition.

Current public record

Netso is building the development, financing and operating system for distributed commercial and industrial rooftop energy in Bangladesh. The public site does not publish customer names, project capacities, development-stage counterparties or commercial terms until the underlying record is approved for disclosure.
