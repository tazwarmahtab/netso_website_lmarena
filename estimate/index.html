Tariffs keep climbing

Buying the plant yourself ties up capital and leaves the owner responsible for performance, maintenance and the asset over its operating life.

Netso removes all three at once: no capital, no operations desk, and a unit of power priced below the grid — settled in a long-term contract, not a purchase.
