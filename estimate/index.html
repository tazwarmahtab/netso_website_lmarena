Bangladesh solar FAQBefore you enquire

Questions facility owners ask first.

How much electricity can rooftop solar generate in Bangladesh?

The calculator uses a regional solar-resource baseline and a screening yield band. Final generation depends on roof geometry, shading, equipment, load profile and survey results.

Which electricity tariff does the calculator use?

The default basis is an 11 kV industrial reference band of Tk 11.56–16.06 per kWh. You can switch to a custom blended rate from your electricity bill.

Does this provide a solar PPA quote?

No. It is an indicative screening tool. A PPA price, term and project size are agreed after a site survey, load review and commercial assessment.
