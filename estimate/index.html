Why now The cost of doing nothing

The grid is taxing the factory.

01

Tariffs keep climbing

Industrial daytime power has moved decisively upward, and every revision lands straight on the production cost of each unit you make.

02

Cuts stop the line

Interruptions halt output. Diesel covers them badly — dirtier, louder, and at a worse price than the grid it replaces.

03

Capital sits on the roof

Buying the plant yourself locks cash on the roof that belongs in production, inventory and growth — for a 25-year payback.

Netso removes all three at once: no capital, no operations desk, and a unit of power priced below the grid — settled in a long-term contract, not a purchase.
