01 — FITWhat makes a site interesting

We qualify the operating reality, not just the roof.

LOAD

Meaningful daytime demand

Solar economics improve when the facility is consuming power while the system is generating. We look at load shape, operating hours and seasonality.

ROOF

Usable industrial roof area

Area is only the starting point. Structure, shading, access, setbacks and electrical topology determine what can actually be built.

COUNTERPARTY

A credible long-term offtaker

The project depends on contracted cash flow. Ownership, payment history, security and decision authority therefore matter early.

Industrial reality

Factories cannot stop because solar is being installed.

The development process has to fit production schedules, roof access, structural constraints, electrical protection, safety requirements and commissioning windows. The website should promise a disciplined process, not a frictionless fantasy.

  • Roof and structural diligence before final sizing
  • Load matching before commercial modelling
  • Electrical integration planned around existing operations
  • Construction and commissioning sequenced around the facility
  • Monitoring and maintenance planned before handover
03 — COMMERCIAL SCREENStart with the information you already have

Your electricity bill is enough to start a conversation.

If you know monthly spend, tariff, roof area and operating hours, the first screen can begin before a site survey.
