01 — THE FINANCING ARCHITECTUREAsset + contract + cash flow

The project is not the panel. It is the contracted energy asset.

Our job is to make each layer legible before capital is committed.

01 · OFFTAKER

Credit + site

Facility, ownership or tenure, consumption profile and payment history.

02 · PPA

Contracted obligation

Tariff, term, metering, billing, default and termination mechanics.

03 · PROJECT SPV

Asset ownership

The project company holds the solar asset and contracts the relevant obligations.

04 · SOLAR ASSET

Generation + O&M

Design, equipment, interconnection, monitoring, maintenance and insurance.

Cash flow

Metered output becomes collections. Collections support operating costs and the agreed debt-service or investor waterfall.

02 — UNDERWRITING STACKWhat we need to prove

Every project has to survive six questions.

01

Can the roof host it?

Usable area, structure, access, shading, electrical topology and interconnection constraints.

02

Can the load absorb it?

Daytime demand, seasonality, sanctioned load and generation profile are matched before sizing.

03

Will the offtaker pay?

Counterparty identity, credit evidence, payment behaviour, security and escalation mechanics.

04

Does the PPA work?

Tariff, term, metering, obligations, default rights and risk allocation have to support the asset.

05

Can the asset perform?

Yield model, equipment specification, O&M plan, monitoring and lifecycle assumptions are documented.

06

Can capital see it?

Diligence, project documents, financial model, reporting and post-close operating data form the evidence trail.
