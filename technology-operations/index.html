Technology + operations

Build the asset.
Run the system.

A rooftop solar project becomes an infrastructure business after commissioning. Netso's operating layer is designed around assets, meters, field work, payments and exceptions.

01 — TECHNICAL SYSTEMFrom roof to meter

Every project has a physical system and a data system.

SITERoof + loadSurvey, structure, shading, electrical and consumption evidence.
ASSETPV + inverter + meterGeneration, protection, monitoring, maintenance and operating records.
OPERATING LAYERMonitor + actPerformance, collections, field exceptions, reporting and escalation.
02 — OPERATING LOOPObserve → decide → act → record
01 · OBSERVE

Telemetry

Generation, availability, meter status and operating signals.

02 · DECIDE

Exceptions

Prioritise faults, arrears, offline assets and field work.

03 · ACT

Field + collections

Technicians and commercial operations resolve the issue.

04 · RECORD

Evidence

The action and outcome become part of the asset record.

What the layer is for

One operating picture across every asset.

  • Asset identity and equipment records
  • Metering and performance evidence
  • Preventive and corrective maintenance
  • Collections status and payment exceptions
  • Field work queues and resolution history
  • Reporting for owners, lenders and counterparties

This page describes the intended operating architecture. It is not a claim that Netso currently operates a live commercial portfolio of these assets.

03 — CAPITAL READINESSOperations create evidence

The goal is not a dashboard. It is a better asset record.

Good operating data reduces ambiguity for the next financing decision, maintenance decision or portfolio review.
